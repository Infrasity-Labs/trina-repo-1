# First week at Pinewharf

Welcome. This is the short version.

## Day 1

1. Sign in to Trina AX and open the **Files** tab. You will see `shared/` and your own team's folder.
2. Read `shared/handbook/company-overview.md` (5 minutes).
3. Read `shared/pricing/price-list-2026.md` and `shared/pricing/discount-policy.md`.
4. Look at the last few quotes in `shared/quotes/` to see how we write them.

## Using the AI

Ask Trina in a chat. It starts from the company's files and from team skills, and it can only open what **you** may open.

- Type `/` to see the skills you can use.
- Sales: use `/quote-from-price-list` for any customer quote request.

## Rules of thumb

- Never promise a price that is not in the price list. Ask Sam.
- The AI's quote is a **draft**. A person reads it before it goes to a customer.
- If something you expect to see is not in Files, you probably do not have access to it. Ask your team lead.
